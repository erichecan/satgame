## 0. 考试事实(全部一手核实)

- **54 题**,分 2 个 module(每个 27 题 = 25 计分 + 2 预试),各 **32 分钟**,共 64 分钟。
- 平均每题 **1.19 分钟**。第二个 module 依第一个表现自适应难度。
- 每题配一段 **25–150 词**的独立短文(按"6 字符 = 1 词"折算),**一段只配一题**。
- 全部为 **四选一**;三个干扰项被官方明确设计为"学生可能犯的常见错误或常见误解"——所以**按分类学排除**是有效的。
- 每个 module 内题目**由易到难**排列。
- 文体四类:**literature、history/social studies、the humanities、science**。不考学科背景知识。
- 图表仅限 **表格、条形图、折线图**;**不需要计算**(该部分不许用计算器)。
- 文本复杂度三档:6–8 年级、9–11 年级、12–14 年级。

# Unit 0 — The Reading Engine

## L0 · The 4-Step Answer Engine
`Skill slug: answer-engine`

**What it looks like** — 不是独立题型,是所有阅读题共用的动作序列。

**Strategy steps**
1. **Read the question first.** 每段只配一题,先知道要找什么,再读文本;不要先通读。
2. **Pull the keywords out of the question.** 具体名词、条件、限定语——它们是回文定位的坐标。
3. **Predict before you look at the options.** 先自己用一句话答出来,再看选项;不预判就会被四个"看起来都对"的选项拖走。
4. **Eliminate by type, not by feel.** 官方明说三个干扰项各自代表一种常见错误,所以逐个问"它属于哪一类错",而不是"哪个读起来顺"。

**Signals** — 无(通用)。

**Common traps** — 全部 8 类都在这里教:same-word / opposite / out-of-scope / too-narrow / too-extreme / fictitious-comparison / non-sequitur / tone-mismatch。

**Worked example sketch** — 给一段科学短文 + 主旨题,故意让"同词复述"选项极具吸引力;正确答案是完整改写。演示"预判 → 逐项归类排除"。

# Unit 1 — Craft and Structure(≈28%,13–15 题)

## L1 · Words in Context ⭐(孩子薄弱点①)
`Skill slug: words-in-context`

**官方定义** — determine the meaning of a high-utility academic word or phrase in context, or use such vocabulary in a precise, contextually appropriate way.

**What it looks like**
- `As used in the text, what does the word "quality" most nearly mean?`
- `Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?`

**Strategy steps(核心:不认识这个词也能做对)**
1. **Cover the options.** 先把四个选项挡住——它们的作用就是把你往常见义上带。
2. **Read the whole sentence and put your own plain word in the blank.** 用最土的词填,比如 "made bigger"、"not sure"。
3. **Fix the direction before the meaning.** 先判正负(褒/贬/中性)和强弱,方向一错整题就废;方向对了通常能直接砍掉两个。
4. **Find the structural clue.** 语境线索只有四种:对比(but/however/unlike/yet)、举例(for example/such as/:)、因果(because/therefore/since)、并列同义(and/;/—)。找到是哪一种,词义方向就定了。
5. **Test each option back in the sentence, not in the abstract.** 高频学术词往往考**次要义**(如 quality、novel、sound、address);"我认识这个词"恰恰是陷阱。

**Signals**
- 对比:but, however, yet, although, unlike, whereas, rather than, in contrast
- 举例:for example, for instance, such as, including, 冒号
- 因果:because, since, therefore, thus, as a result, consequently
- 同义并列:and, similarly, likewise, 分号, 破折号

**Common traps** — `same-word`(选项就是文中原词的常见义)、`tone-mismatch`(意思对但褒贬反了)、`too-extreme`(强度过头)、`opposite`。

**Worked example sketch** — 一段带 "but" 的短文,空格前后语义反转;正确答案与常见义无关,只与句内对比关系一致。演示"先定方向、再定词义"。

## L2 · Text Structure and Purpose
`Skill slug: text-structure-purpose`

**官方定义** — analyze the structure of a text or determine the main rhetorical purpose of a text.

**What it looks like**
- `Which choice best states the main purpose of the text?`
- `Which choice best states the function of the underlined sentence in the text as a whole?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Ask "what is the author DOING," not "what is the text ABOUT."** purpose ≠ main idea。
2. **Read the options as verb + object.** 选项都是动作短语(to argue that…, to illustrate…, to trace…),先看动词对不对。
3. **Weight the first and last sentence,** 再看 but/however 之后的半段——转折之后往往才是作者的真正立场。
4. **For "function of the underlined sentence," ask what it does FOR the sentences around it**(举例?让步?引出反驳?),而不是它本身说了什么。
5. **Match the tone.** 作者是中立描述还是提出主张?动词强度要匹配。

**Signals** — 动词区分:describe / argue / illustrate / trace / compare / critique / propose;转折 but, however, yet, although;让步 admittedly, to be sure, granted。

**Common traps** — `too-narrow`(拿一个细节当全文目的)、`same-word`(复述内容而不是说明功能)、`too-extreme`(把"描述"说成"证明")。

**Worked example sketch** — 一段先陈述通行看法、再用 however 引出新证据的短文;问 main purpose。正确答案的动词必须涵盖"转折"这个动作,只覆盖前半段的选项是 too-narrow。

## L3 · Cross-Text Connections
`Skill slug: cross-text-connections`

**官方定义** — draw a reasonable connection between two topically related texts.

**What it looks like**
- `Based on the texts, how would … (Text 2) most likely describe the view of … presented in Text 1?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Reduce each text to one sentence: claim + stance.** 两段各写一句"谁认为什么"。
2. **Name the relationship before reading the options:** 同意 / 反对 / 部分同意需补充 / 换角度 / 提供证据。
3. **Anchor on Text 2's attitude,** 因为问法几乎总是"Text 2 的作者会怎么看 Text 1"。
4. **Check the option's strength word.** 官方最爱用"部分同意 + 需修正"这类温和选项做正解,而把"完全否定/完全支持"做成 too-extreme 干扰项。

**Signals** — Text 2 里的态度词:however, in fact, contrary to, supports, complicates, refines, overlooks。

**Common traps** — `too-extreme`(把"需要修正"写成"完全错误")、`opposite`(把两段关系说反)、`out-of-scope`(引入两段都没有的第三方观点)。

**Worked example sketch** — Text 1 给理论预测,Text 2 的实验结果与预测不完全一致;正解是"largely correct but needs refinement",干扰项是"not compelling regardless of data"(too-extreme)。

# Unit 2 — Information and Ideas(≈26%,12–14 题)

## L4 · Central Ideas and Details
`Skill slug: central-ideas-details`

**官方定义** — determine the central idea of a text and/or interpret the key details supporting that idea.

**What it looks like** — `Which choice best states the main idea of the text?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Decide first: main idea or a detail?** 主旨题的答案必须覆盖全段;细节题的答案只需对应一句。
2. **For main idea, cover the whole arc.** 若段落有转折,答案必须包含转折后的内容。
3. **For details, go back and locate.** 用题干关键词定位那一句,答案是它的改写。
4. **The correct answer paraphrases; it does not echo.** 用原词最多的选项通常是陷阱。

**Signals** — 主旨常落在首句或末句;`but / however / yet` 之后是重心;`therefore / in short / ultimately` 引出结论。

**Common traps** — `too-narrow`(细节冒充主旨)、`same-word`、`out-of-scope`、`too-extreme`。

**Worked example sketch** — 三句短文:通行看法 → however → 新发现 + 意义。正确答案覆盖"新发现推翻旧看法";干扰项只讲旧看法(too-narrow)。

## L5 · Command of Evidence — Textual
`Skill slug: evidence-textual`

**官方定义** — use textual evidence (a fact, detail, or example) to support, challenge, or otherwise respond to a specified claim.

**What it looks like**
- `Which quotation from "…" most effectively illustrates the claim?`
- `Which finding, if true, would most strongly support …?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Underline the claim word for word.** 证据题的胜负全在"claim 到底说了什么",不是文章说了什么。
2. **Decide the job: support, challenge, or illustrate.** 三种要求的正确答案方向完全不同。
3. **Restate the claim as a testable sentence,** 再问每个选项:它是否**直接**让这句话更可信?
4. **Reject options that are merely true.** 干扰项常常是文中真话,但与 claim 无关——"真"不等于"支持"。
5. **For "if true" questions, treat every option as fact** 并只比较相关性。

**Signals** — claim 里的限定语:only, primarily, because, more than, before;题干动词 support / undermine / illustrate / challenge。

**Common traps** — `out-of-scope`(真但不相关)、`opposite`(实际削弱了 claim)、`too-narrow`(只支持 claim 的一半)、`same-word`。

**Worked example sketch** — 假设"帆状背脊用于水中推进";四个模型实验结果里,只有一个直接对应"水中运动效率"。演示"真 ≠ 支持"。

## L6 · Command of Evidence — Quantitative ⭐(孩子薄弱点②之一)
`Skill slug: evidence-quantitative`

**官方定义** — use quantitative evidence (data from an informational graphic) to support, challenge, or otherwise respond to a claim。图表仅限**表格/条形图/折线图**,且**不需要计算**。

**What it looks like** — `Which choice most effectively uses data from the table to complete the example?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Read the question, then the sentence with the blank — before the chart.** 先知道要证明什么。
2. **Read the chart's furniture first:** 标题、坐标轴、单位、图例。单位读错是这类题最贵的错误。
3. **Name the pattern in your own words:** 上升 / 下降 / 某组明显高低 / 两组差距大。
4. **Match the claim's strength to the data's strength.** 如果句子说 "varied widely",答案必须体现**大**差距,而不只是"不同"。
5. **Verify every number in the option against the chart.** 选项常把数字张冠李戴——数字对但配错了类别。

**Signals** — varied widely / whereas / by contrast / the most / the least / more than double。

**Common traps** — `opposite`(读反趋势)、`fictitious-comparison`(比较图里没有并列的两项)、`out-of-scope`(用了图上没有的数据)、`too-narrow`(只覆盖部分区间却用来概括整体)。

**Worked example sketch** — 一张按职业分组的百分比表 + 一句 "evaluations varied widely, for example ___";正解并列最高与最低两项;干扰项取两个数值相近的项(不构成 "widely")。

## L7 · Inferences
`Skill slug: inferences`

**官方定义** — draw a reasonable inference based on explicit and/or implicit information and ideas in a text.

**What it looks like** — `Which choice most logically completes the text?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Read to the blank and stop.** 空格几乎总在末尾,前面是完整的推理链。
2. **Ask what the author is building toward**(exigence):这段为什么要写?
3. **Say your own ending out loud before reading the options.**
4. **The inference must be NOT stated.** 直接复述文中原话的选项一定错——那是复述,不是推断。
5. **Kill the extremes.** 正确的推断通常带 likely / may / suggests;带 will / all / never / prove 的基本可弃。

**Signals** — 空格前的 `This suggests that ___` / `Therefore, ___` / `so ___`;文中让步语 although, despite。

**Common traps** — `too-extreme`、`non-sequitur`(跳步)、`same-word`(复述)、`out-of-scope`(引入外部常识)。

**Worked example sketch** — 雪豹生活在高海拔、代谢成本高、竞争者少;空格填"因此它们迁往高处的原因";正解是"低处竞争激烈把它们挤上去",干扰项含"雪豹一定比其他猫科更强"(too-extreme)。

# Unit 3 — Expression of Ideas(≈20%,8–12 题)

## L8 · Transitions
`Skill slug: transitions`

**官方定义** — determine the most effective transition word or phrase to logically connect information and ideas.

**What it looks like** — `Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Cover the options first.** 过渡词题看选项就会被"哪个顺口"骗。
2. **Read the sentence before and the sentence after,** 各压缩成一个短句。
3. **Name the relationship in plain words:** 同向补充 / 反向对比 / 因果 / 举例说明 / 时间顺序。
4. **Only then pick the word matching that relationship.** 每类过渡词是可互换的一组,先定类别再挑词。
5. **Beware "however" reflex.** 前后不构成对立时,however 就是错的。

**Signals(四类 + 时间序)**
- 同向补充:moreover, furthermore, in addition, similarly, likewise
- 反向对比:however, nevertheless, in contrast, on the other hand, still, conversely
- 因果:therefore, thus, consequently, as a result, hence
- 举例说明:for example, for instance, specifically, in particular
- 时间/顺序:meanwhile, subsequently, ultimately, then

**Common traps** — `opposite`(该同向却给反向)、`non-sequitur`(给因果但前后无因果)。

**Worked example sketch** — 前句说预报很糟,后句说照常出发 → 让步/对比关系,选 Nevertheless;Therefore 是把关系装反(opposite)。

## L9 · Rhetorical Synthesis
`Skill slug: rhetorical-synthesis`

**官方定义** — strategically integrate provided information and ideas to form an effective sentence achieving a specified rhetorical aim.

**What it looks like**
- 一组 bullet 笔记 + `The student wants to emphasize a similarity between the two works. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Read the GOAL sentence first and underline the goal verb**(emphasize a similarity / contrast / explain / introduce)。
2. **Ignore the notes until you know the goal.** 笔记是素材库,不是要点。
3. **Test each option against the goal only.** 四个选项通常都是笔记里的真话——唯一标准是"有没有完成那个任务"。
4. **A "similarity" goal needs both items joined by a similarity word;** "contrast" 目标需要 however/whereas 这类标记。语法通顺不算完成任务。

**Signals** — 目标动词:emphasize, introduce, explain, compare, contrast, describe;完成相似性的标记:both, like, similarly, share。

**Common traps** — `too-narrow`(只用了一个来源却要求比较两者)、`opposite`(目标要相似却写成对比)、`out-of-scope`(加了笔记里没有的内容)。

**Worked example sketch** — 两件雕塑作品的材料笔记;目标是"强调相似";正解用 "Like … both … use discarded objects";干扰项用 however 列出差异(opposite)。

# Unit 4 — Standard English Conventions(≈26%,11–15 题)

## L10 · Boundaries
`Skill slug: boundaries`

**官方定义** — edit text to ensure that sentences are conventionally complete.

**What it looks like** — `Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Ignore meaning. This is a structure question.** 只看两侧各是不是完整句。
2. **Test both sides:** 左边能独立成句吗?右边呢?
3. **Apply the rule table:**
   - 独立句 + 独立句 → 句号 / 分号 / `, and`(逗号必须带连词) / 冒号(右边解释左边)
   - 独立句 + 非独立句 → 逗号或不加标点,**绝不能**用分号
   - **逗号单独连接两个完整句 = comma splice,永远错**
4. **Colon rule:** 冒号左边必须是完整句,右边可以是清单/解释/强调。
5. **Never split subject from its verb with a single comma.**

**Signals** — 选项之间只差标点(`, / ; / : / —`)就是 Boundaries 题;含 however/therefore 且前面是逗号,多半是 comma splice 陷阱。

**Common traps** — 这类题不走 8 类语义分类学,错因是结构:comma splice、分号接从句、冒号左边不完整。

**Worked example sketch** — 两个完整句被逗号连接;四个选项分别为逗号(错,splice)、分号(对)、无标点(错,run-on)、冒号(错,右边不是解释)。

## L11 · Form, Structure, and Sense
`Skill slug: form-structure-sense`

**官方定义** — edit text to conform to conventional usage (e.g., agreement, verb tense/aspect).

**What it looks like** — `Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?`(与 Boundaries 同题干,靠选项差异区分)

**Strategy steps**
1. **Look at what changes across the options.** 变的是动词形式?代词?就按那一项判。
2. **Subject–verb agreement: strip the modifiers first.** 把介词短语和从句删掉,主语才露出来(单复数常被中间的短语掩盖)。
3. **Verb tense: find the time anchor in the sentence**(in 1892 / currently / by the time …),时态跟锚点走。
4. **Pronouns need one unambiguous antecedent,** 且单复数一致。
5. **Modifiers must sit next to what they modify;** 句首分词短语的逻辑主语必须是紧随其后的主语。
6. **Redundancy:** 若选项把已说过的信息再说一遍,删掉那个信息的最短选项通常对。

**Signals** — 选项只在动词/代词/词序上变化;句首出现分词短语("Having studied…");出现 each / every / neither(取单数)。

**Common traps** — 结构类错误为主:主谓不一致、时态漂移、悬垂修饰、代词指代不明、冗余。

**Worked example sketch** — 主语与动词之间插一个长介词短语,让复数名词紧挨动词;正解按真正的单数主语选单数动词。

# Unit 5 — Passage-Type Playbook ⭐(孩子薄弱点②;本课程自建,非官方分类)

> 官方只规定四个 subject area:literature、history/social studies、the humanities、science,且明确**不考学科背景知识**。
> 本单元教的是"当学科换了,读法怎么调",不是补学科知识。

## L12 · Science Passages
`Skill slug: science-passages`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Map the passage onto the research skeleton:** 背景 → 假设/问题 → 方法 → 结果 → 局限/意义。25–150 词的短文通常只出现其中 2–3 环,先判断出现的是哪几环。
2. **Find the hypothesis sentence and mark it.** 科学类题几乎都围绕"这个假设成不成立"。
3. **Separate what was MEASURED from what was CONCLUDED.** 干扰项最常把结论说成观测结果。
4. **Don't supply outside science knowledge.** 官方明说不考背景知识——文中没说就是没说。
5. **Technical nouns don't need to be understood.** 把不认识的术语当成代号 X,关系读懂即可。

**Signals** — hypothesized, predicted, observed, measured, suggests, may indicate, however, although;局限词 limited, small sample, further research。

**Common traps** — `out-of-scope`(用课外科学常识)、`too-extreme`(把 suggests 读成 proves)、`opposite`(把结果方向读反)。

## L13 · History & Social Studies Passages
`Skill slug: history-social-studies`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Identify whose voice it is:** 历史当事人的原话,还是后世研究者的分析?两者态度层次不同。
2. **For older documents, translate to plain modern English first,** 一句一句转述,再回答。古旧措辞是这类题真正的难点。
3. **Track the argument, not the era.** 找主张、让步、反驳三个位置。
4. **Watch inverted and long sentences:** 找主语和主动词,其余先当修饰扔掉(与 Trim 同一个动作)。
5. **Attitude questions ask about the AUTHOR's stance,** 不是文中被引述者的立场。

**Signals** — 让步 notwithstanding, albeit, howsoever, to be sure;主张 ought, must, it is the duty of;后世分析 historians argue, recent scholarship。

**Common traps** — `tone-mismatch`(误判作者态度)、`same-word`(被古语原词吸引)、`out-of-scope`(套用课本上的历史背景)。

## L14 · Humanities Passages
`Skill slug: humanities-passages`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Locate the evaluative sentence** —— 人文类短文多在描述一部作品/一位创作者,其中总有一句是评价。
2. **Separate description from judgment.** 主旨与 purpose 题的答案通常挂在判断句上。
3. **Read the tone words as data.** 形容词的褒贬就是作者立场的证据。
4. **When an artist/work is unfamiliar, ignore identity and keep the relationship**(谁影响谁、谁反对谁)。

**Signals** — 评价词 innovative, derivative, understated, deceptively simple;转折 yet, still, even so。

**Common traps** — `tone-mismatch`、`too-extreme`(把温和赞赏写成极力推崇)、`too-narrow`(拿一处描述当全文主旨)。

## L15 · Literature & Poetry
`Skill slug: literature-poetry`

**Strategy steps**
1. **Answer the plot question first:** 谁,在哪,发生了什么。先把字面读通再谈手法。
2. **For poetry, read by sentence, not by line.** 跨行不代表断句,按标点重组。
3. **Anchor on the speaker's feeling and whether it changes.** 情感转折点通常就是考点。
4. **Figurative language still needs textual proof.** 象征义必须在文中有落点,不能自由联想。
5. **For "which quotation illustrates the claim," match the claim's emotion, not its topic.**

**Signals** — 情感转折 but, yet, until, no longer, once;文学分析词 speaker, imagery, tone, shift。

**Common traps** — `out-of-scope`(过度解读象征)、`opposite`(把情感方向读反)、`too-narrow`(只覆盖一节)、`tone-mismatch`。
